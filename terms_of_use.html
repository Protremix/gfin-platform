1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the Global Fraud Intelligence Network (GFIN) platform at gfin-system.com, you agree to these Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. Platform Description

GFIN is an international law enforcement platform that enables citizens to report fraud and scams, and provides law enforcement agencies with intelligence tools for investigation and prevention. GFIN connects 189 countries through automated case routing to national cybercrime authorities, Interpol, and Europol.

3. User Types

  • Victims/Citizens: May register, file complaints, upload evidence, and track complaint status
  • Police/Law Enforcement: Authorized personnel with authenticated access to investigation tools and case data
  • General Public: May search the scam database and access awareness materials without registration

4. Complaint Guidelines

  • All complaints must be truthful and filed in good faith
  • Deliberately filing false complaints is a criminal offense in most jurisdictions
  • Provide accurate and complete information to the best of your knowledge
  • Evidence files must be authentic and unaltered
  • Complaints are treated as allegations until corroborated by investigation

5. Prohibited Activities

  • Filing false or malicious complaints
  • Uploading malware, viruses, or malicious files
  • Attempting to access police dashboards without authorization
  • Scraping or automated data extraction from the platform
  • Attempting to disrupt or compromise platform security
  • Using the platform for any illegal purpose
