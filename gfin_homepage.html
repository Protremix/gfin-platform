Public Awareness

Know the Signs — Protect Yourself

GFIN tracks 15 categories of fraud. Learn how to recognize and avoid each type.

💸
Recovery Scams

Someone promises to recover money you lost? They're scamming you again.

❤️
Romance Scams

Online relationship leads to requests for money, crypto, or gifts.

📈
Investment Fraud

Guaranteed returns, pressure to act fast, fake trading platforms.

🎣
Phishing

Fake emails, SMS, or websites designed to steal your credentials.

🎭
Impersonation

Someone pretends to be from your bank, government, or tech support.

₿
Crypto Fraud

Fake giveaways, mining schemes, and wallet-draining contracts.

💻
Tech Support Scams

"Your computer is infected" — fake support agents want remote access.

💰
Advance Fee Scams

Pay upfront for a loan, prize, or service that never materializes.

💼
Job Scams

Work-from-home offers requiring you to pay for training or equipment.
